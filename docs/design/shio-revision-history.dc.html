WHAT THIS DECIDES
The diff is per field, not per document, and that is a backend decision as much as a layout one: it is what SH989 needs to say which fields an approved run touched, and it is why a revision has to store a field map rather than a rendered blob.
NOT CHOSEN — A DIFF PAGE OF ITS OWN
