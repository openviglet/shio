THE DISCRIMINATOR
Does the reader arrive to compare many, or to pick one? Comparing is rows, and rows are the default: the eye runs down a column, and a tile that has to be scanned against six others is a tile the reader is fighting. Picking is tiles, which a page asks for: media, blueprints, a hub, a handful told apart by sight.
