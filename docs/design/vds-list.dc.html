THE DISCRIMINATOR
Does the reader arrive to pick one, or to compare many? Picking is tiles: the chip carries identity, the span carries weight, and eight of them fill a screen. Comparing is rows: the eye runs down a column, and a tile that has to be scanned against six others is a tile the reader is fighting.
WHY IT IS WORTH SAYING
Without it, density reads as a reason to drop out of the layer and hand-roll a card and a table -- which is how one product ends up with two eras of chrome on one screen.
