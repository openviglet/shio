WHAT CHANGED, BY PIN
1
Grid or list, remembered per person. Grid is the default up to about thirty sites, where a picture beats a name; past that, list. The choice is a per-viewer preference, not shared state.
2
The thumbnail is the home page. Rendered by the preview renderer when the home is published, and stored. Opening the list renders nothing.
3
No home, no fake picture. A site without a home page shows its initials and says so, the same honesty as “No pages yet”.
4
The list row’s facts, no more. Address, pages by state, last change and who made it. Switching view never changes what is known.
5
Focus is visible on both grounds. The focused card carries the ring and the same verbs as a hovered row: Preview, then ···.
On this ground the primary is orange-400 with dark text, as --vg-primary-base-dark already sets. The state colours are lifted one step so they keep contrast on #141414.
WHAT THIS DECIDES
A thumbnail is an artefact of publishing, not of viewing: one render of the home when it is published, kept beside the render digest, and served as an image. Drawn here as a card per site, it commits the backend to producing and storing that image; without it the grid falls back to the initials tile for every site, and the list is the better view.
NOT CHOSEN — A LIVE PREVIEW IN EACH CARD
Twenty-three iframes, each a full server render with its scripts, on every visit to the list. Slow, unreadable to a screen reader, and showing drafts as if they were the site. A stored image says the same thing for the cost of one request.
