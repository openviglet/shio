WHAT THE SHAPE DECIDES
EACH PANE SCROLLS ON ITS OWN
The fields and what they change stay in view together. The hero and the save bar sit above both, so neither pane owns them.
THE READER OWNS THE RATIO
Dragged at the handle, and remembered per viewer under storageKey. It is a convenience, so storage that is blocked only means it starts at the default.
THE SECOND PANE NEVER VANISHES
At phone width the two become one with a labelled switch. Nothing drops off the edge of a narrow screen unannounced.
NOT A GRID OF YOUR OWN
Two columns in a page's own grid settle none of the three decisions above, and each page settles them differently.
