WHY NOT
A tray is a selection, and three filed lines need a record. SH993 wants a unit to stage, preview, approve, schedule and undo — a schedule needs a row that outlives the session. SH994 wants a rollback anchor per item, which has to be written when the release publishes, not when a tab is open. SH998 wants a waiver with a reason, attached to something a webhook can name.
WHAT IT WOULD HAVE COST TO FIND OUT LATER
The tray is two days of console work and no backend. The gap only appears when the first schedule is set, by which time the console has a shape that assumes no entity exists — and the cheapest fix from there is to keep the tray and bolt a table behind it, which is how a product ends up with both.
