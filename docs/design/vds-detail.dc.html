Notice what is not on the bar: the delete. A controlled dialog rendered in both halves opens two modals, and an action in the fade-out group vanishes as the reader scrolls.
IDENTITY LIVES IN THE HERO, NEVER IN THE FORM
Title, description, icon and enabled state are the shell's. A form field for any of them is a second place to edit one thing, and the two will disagree about which was saved.
THE SAVE BAR IS ALWAYS THE MORPH
Controls start in the hero and the fixed bar fades in as the hero leaves, so nothing is duplicated on screen and the bar arrives exactly when the title goes. Never a hand-rolled sticky div, and never a bar that is always there.
