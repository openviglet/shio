shio notes — what a curator is still asking for, across every site

  --site <name>        Scope to one site
  --kind <kind>        request, comment or reply
  --limit <n>          How many rows
  --strict             Exit 1 while anything is open (CI)
  --json               Raw JSON instead of lines

Examples:
  shio notes --kind request
  shio notes --site Acme --strict
