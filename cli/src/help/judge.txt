shio judge --site <name> — run the instruments, answer once, name the disagreements

verify, diff and audit run (audit whenever playwright resolves). snapshot is opt-in:
choosing what to compare pixels with is a judgement. A skipped instrument reads NOT RUN,
never green. Findings are judged against acceptance.properties: at or below the bar the
round is ACCEPTED and they print as tasks for later; above it, or unnamed there, they
HOLD and this exits 1.

  --site <name>        The site to judge (or a bare positional)
  --fidelity           Hold pixels to 0.00% (default authorable: not a criterion there)
  --no-browser         Skip audit even where playwright is installed (--browser forces it)
  --against <origin>   Also run snapshot against this origin (the source you replicated)
  --pages <n>          How many pages audit/snapshot look at (default 5)
  --verbose            Print each instrument's own report underneath the judgement
  --json               The judgement as JSON, for a pipeline

Examples:
  shio judge --site Acme
  shio judge --site Acme --against https://acme.example.com --fidelity
