shio rollback — put a captured revision back into the draft

  --address <addr>     The post (or the first argument)
  --to <n>             The revision number, from `shio versions`
  --apply              Write it; without this it only plans
  --json               Raw JSON instead of lines

Examples:
  shio rollback post:Blog/blog/hello-world --to 3
  shio rollback post:Blog/blog/hello-world --to 3 --apply

It lands in the draft: `shio publish` is the separate act.
