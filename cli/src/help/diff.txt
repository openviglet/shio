shio diff --site <name> - compare what a replica serves with what declared it

Reads the served bytes with the capture's own scanner: counts, not pixels. Exits 1 when a
page lost something; a fact the replica GAINED is never a failure.

  --site <name>        The site the replica lives in (required)
  --against <what>     Which declaration to judge against (default: capture)
                       capture   - the served page against the source
                       proposal  - the instance's content against the conversion plan
                       published - the draft against what is live
                       revision  - the draft against a revision (--address, --from)
  --from <n>           The revision (--against revision); `shio versions` lists them
  --address <address>  Narrow --against published to these posts (repeatable), or name the
                       post for --against revision; without it the answer is the site's
  --plan <file>        Read the accepted decisions from somewhere else (--against proposal)
  --proposal <file>    Read the candidates from somewhere else (--against proposal)
  --mode <how>         What the counts claim: auto (default), fidelity, mixed, authorable
  --inventory <file>   Read a capture from somewhere else
  --limit <n>          Pages to print
  --json               The comparison as JSON on stdout instead of the report

Examples:
  shio diff --site Acme
  shio diff --site Acme --against proposal
  shio diff --site Acme --mode fidelity
  shio diff --site Acme --against published --address post:Acme/blog/hello-world
