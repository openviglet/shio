shio export site:<name> — hand a whole site to another instance, as a zip

  --site <name>        The site, when no positional is given
  --out <file>         Where to write it (default: the name the server chose)
  --json               The path and size as JSON

Carries the site, its folders, its posts in both states, their post-types and the site's
notes — not users, tokens, revisions, releases or uploaded bytes. `shio import` is the
other half.

Example:
  shio export site:Acme --out acme.zip
