shio review — what an agent run left for a person, and what to do about it

  list                 One line per run: its intent, its counts, what is waiting
  show <session>       That run's changes, with whatever has been decided
  decide <session>     Approve, reject or hold named changes
  mine                 What a curator decided about your own runs

  --approve <a,b>      Publish these (address or post id)
  --reject <a,b>       Undo these; needs --note
  --hold <a,b>         Park these; the queue stops counting them
  --note "<why>"       Why — required with --reject
  --dry-run            Print what it would do, change nothing
  --strict             Exit 1 while anything is waiting, or was rejected (CI)
  --decision <kind>    mine: reject (default), approve or hold
  --limit <n>          How many rows
  --json               Raw JSON instead of lines

Examples:
  shio review list --strict
  shio review mine --strict
  shio review show 7f3c9a2e
  shio review decide 7f3c9a2e --approve post:Blog/about --hold post:Blog/pricing
  shio review decide 7f3c9a2e --reject post:Blog/news --note "prices are last quarter's"
