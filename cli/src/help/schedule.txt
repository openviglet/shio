shio schedule — what goes live or comes down, in time order

  --site <name>        One site (or the first argument)
  --from <instant>     ISO-8601; entries at or after it
  --to <instant>       ISO-8601; entries before it
  --json               The entries as JSON

Examples:
  shio schedule Blog
  shio schedule --from 2026-09-15T00:00:00Z --to 2026-09-22T00:00:00Z
