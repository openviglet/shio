About Viglet

Our mission is to simplify content management

Founded with a vision to democratize content management, Viglet brings enterprise-grade CMS capabilities to teams of all sizes. Our open-source platform is built on modern technologies like Spring Boot and React.

We believe that content management should be accessible, flexible, and enjoyable. That's why we built the Universal Editor — an inline editing experience that puts content authors in control.
